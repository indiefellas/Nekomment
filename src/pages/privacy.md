# Privacy Policy

Nekomment collects these information to provide our Service, and to prevent abuse to our Service.

We do not collect these to send targeted advertisements or to sell your information to third parties.

## Information we Collect

- The information you put into your Nekomment Account, such as your E-mail address, and username.
- Any comments you sent into a Nekomment-powered comment section.
- Your IP address, and browser information.

## How that information is used

- These information are used to provide our Service to you.
- These information are also used to moderate and to prevent abuse on our Service.
- Nekomment does not sell your information to a third-party.
- Nekomment does not look at your comments unless in case of suspected abuse or a legal order.

## Changes to this policy

We reserve the right to change our policy at any time. By using this Service, you agree to be bound by the terms of this policy.
